# Stakeholder Communication Guide

## Principles
- Lead with the decision, not the context.
- Be direct about impact (timeline, cost, scope).
- Avoid technical jargon unless the audience is technical.
- End with a clear call to action or next step.

---

## Template: Scope Change Notification

**Subject:** [Project Name] – Scope Change Notification: [Change Title]

> Hi [Stakeholder Name],
>
> I wanted to update you on a scope change we've assessed for [Project Name].
>
> **What changed:** [Brief description]
> **Why:** [Business rationale]
> **Impact:** [Timeline / budget / resource effect]
> **Our decision:** [Accept / Defer / Reject] — [one-line reason]
>
> Next steps: [Action items and owner]
>
> Please let me know if you have questions.
>
> [Your name], Product Owner

---

## Template: Priority Reprioritization Notice

**Subject:** [Project Name] – Updated Priority: [Topic]

> Hi [Team / Stakeholder],
>
> Following our review, we've updated the priority order for the current sprint/release.
>
> **New priority:** [Item] moves to [position] because [reason].
> **Impact:** [What gets pushed or deprioritized]
>
> This decision aligns with our goal of [business objective].
>
> [Your name], Product Owner

## Template: Vision Alignment Memo

**Subject:** [Project Name] – Product Vision & Objectives Recap

> Hi team,
>
> As we move into [phase/sprint], I wanted to realign on our product vision and what success looks like.
>
> **Vision:** [One sentence]
> **Top 3 Objectives:**
> 1. [Objective 1]
> 2. [Objective 2]
> 3. [Objective 3]
>
> **What's in scope:** [Key items]
> **What's not:** [Explicit exclusions]
>
> Let's keep these north stars in mind as we make day-to-day decisions.
>
> [Your name], Product Owner
